## Description

Broadcast on Radiophrenia 17 April 2025 7:00 am - 8:00 am

A longform audio representation of a year spent walking with theraputic intent. Cogitare Deambulare - Derived from the latin words "Cogitare" - to think, and Deambulare - To walk - Think Walk. Walking for the purpose of thinking.

The piece is the result of a year spent walking, listening, thinking, recording and making some sounds. All put together in one audio pot. After a Life changing event happened, I found myself walking a lot as a way of processing what had happened.

Somewhere during this journey, I acquired a Border collie puppy. This gave another meaning to these walks. They were no longer simply therapy, they were now a necessity. This dog had to be walked!

## Description

Triptych 3 is part 3 of the Tryptich Trilogy. Each piece comprises of three long-form improvisational guitar pieces, each made up of three distinct sections recorded live in the North-West of Ireland, Summer, 2021.

The aim was to perform the pieces in one go - no overdubs, nothing pre-planned.

Recorded in late Summer evenings as the sun went down, the pieces were born from a kind of meditation. Exploring the sensation of getting lost in sound. Allowing yourself to linger in a moment and in no way rushing to the inevitable end.

Utilising software to loop, twist and manipulate the guitar's signal in real time creating lush, organic soundscapes with a digital twist. The pieces explore the fragile and the temporary. Loops are created, developed and then fade away, never to return. Warm, pad-like swells dance around the stereo image and alternate between ethereal drones, momentary glitches and pitch-shifted motifs.

Mat Warren takes you on an immersive audio journey that is best listened to with closed eyes and an open mind.

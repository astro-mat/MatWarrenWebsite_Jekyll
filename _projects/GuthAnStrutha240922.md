## Description
---
A meditation on the river this live site specific performance by Undercurrent takes the form of three duets between musicians and dancers. Performed live to audiences at Carrick-on-Shannon boardwalk for Culture Night 2022. Supported by Leitrim Live Performance Program. 
Created and performed by dancers Isolde O'Brolchain Carmody, Gaelin Little and Robin Wilson with musicians Natalia Beylis, Milo Cummins and Mat Warren. Artistic direction by Kate Wilson. Film work by Stuie Cambell with additional camera by Jonas Dellow.

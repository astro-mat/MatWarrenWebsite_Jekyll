## Description
"Cogitare Deambulare" was broadcast as part of Radiophrenia on 17 April 2025.

The title derives from the Latin words *cogitare* (to think) and *deambulare* (to walk), translating roughly as "think walk" or walking for the purpose of thinking.

The piece is a longform sound collage created from a year spent walking, listening, thinking, recording and making. Following a significant life event, walking became a way of processing and understanding what had happened. Field recordings, environmental sounds and composed material gathered during this period were gradually assembled into a single audio work.

During this time, the arrival of a Border Collie puppy gave the walks another purpose. What had begun as a therapeutic practice became a daily necessity. The resulting piece is both a record of movement through landscapes and a document of a year of reflection, routine and change.

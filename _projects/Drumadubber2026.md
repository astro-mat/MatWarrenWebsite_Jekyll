## Description
---
A small, intimate in-house live performance for Drumnadubber arts, presenting improvised music on modular synthesizer. With other performances by Risteárd Ó hAodha and Willie Stewart

The performance Marked my first performance with just a modular synthesizer and the first performance in the newly opened Library at Drumerdubber arts space.

## Patch Notes
---
A four voice patch mixed through a Behringer Four play into a Make Noise Mimiophon. Main clock comes from cycling left side of the Make noise maths into a Pamelas new workout clock input.

Voice 1:  
Music Thing Turing Machine into an ornament and crime quantermain mode. Clock from the turing machine went into a mult. Output from channel 1 of ornament and crime went into another mult and then into a make noise DPO 2nd oscillator 1V/oct input. The final output of this went into a Make Noise optomix V1 and the output into channel 1 of the Four Play. The control input of the optomix was fed from the envelope out of the 2nd half of a Make Noise Maths, triggered by the clock mult.

Voice 2: 
Make noise QPAS pinged by Pamelas New Workout (channel 2 output, clock multiplier to x4, wave set to Gate, waveform set to trigger, Width 10%,	EStep 16, ETrig	5, ERot	0,	RSkip 0%.). Bandpass output, frequency modulated by a random Pams output. all into channel 2 of the Four Play

Voice 3: 
Channel 2 of the Ornament and crime into the V/oct input of mutable plaits set to high hat percussion mode. Trigger from random pams output and also the morph. Output into Channel 3 of the Four Play.

Voice 4:
Make noise Morphagene with custom reel. Only played the 1st splice which was Richard Burtons recording of ["under milk wood" by Dylan Thomas](https://open.spotify.com/album/5N76qTciUzKcQuQZ8Dga6m?si=GrQ9sdVbR9q7jF6qudymHQ)

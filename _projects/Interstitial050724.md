## Description

in·​ter·​sti·​tial ˌin-tər-ˈsti-shəl

"occurring in or being an interval or intervening space or segment. of, relating to, or forming an interstice"

"Its about exploring the space between elements, the forgotten realms between instances, the threads that connect different states" Says Mat, inviting us on a musical odyssey through his latest album, "interstitial". In 48 minutes, the listener is transported through a multiverse of sound. whirling and rotating, expanding and contracting.

"Interstitial" builds upon Mat's previous releases - the ambitious longform musings of "Tryptich, and the dreamy, more 'radio-friendly' audio sketchings of "Nephoscope" - this latest album marks a further descent into darkness. A journey away from safety and towards a more disjointed, angular and often disturbing path to the places between places.

Composed, performed and recorded during the winter of 2024 at the recently completed Studio Moo Moo in the northwest of Ireland. During short, rare spouts of creativity after some life altering changes in circumstance and severe existential Crises, "interstitial" explores those gaps that exist between the normal, Between awake and asleep, happy and sad, loud and quiet, clean and dirty. Not everything is so easy to seperate. There is often an inbetween place seldom explored. Often untouched perhaps for reasons best left un-investigated.

Every element you hear is coaxed from an electric guitar, twisted and morphed and looped through a series of modular synthesizers, Delays, loopers, filters, distortions, granularisers and glitch effects.

Pursuing a darker trajectory than his previous releases, "Interstitial" finds Mat straying further from tradition guitar sounds than his previous releases. Relying more on ambient pillows of creepy loops and feedback drones that extend to uncomfortable extremes. Giving way to more expected, reverb drenched guitar lines emerging atop glitchy spurts of noise.

Part improvised noodlings, part considered composition, the resulting tracks were recorded with minimal post-processing presenting a true representation of a time and place. "Interstitial" is music for mind journeys and boundless introspection: meditations on the infinite nature of time and space.
